Privacy

Effective August 26, 2026

Lingua Relay is operated by the javin23863 Lingua Relay project.

What the app processes

Lingua Relay processes microphone audio to create live captions and translations. Camera and natural call audio travel between participants through encrypted WebRTC connections and may use a relay when a direct connection is unavailable.

Where processing happens

Cloudflare provides the room, signalling, relay, account service, and private abuse-report inbox. Modal runs speech recognition, translation, and optional translated-voice processing.

What we keep

The service has no advertising profile, transcript history, conversation database, guest profile, or server-side participant-block history. Audio, video, captions, chat text, and translated voice are not intentionally stored as conversation history. A host-control token, language choices, one random pseudonymous participant-safety ID, and a bounded list of blocked participant-safety IDs may be kept on the user's device. The safety ID is not an account identifier, contact identifier, advertising identifier, or credential. It exists only so the device can recognize a participant it previously blocked. During room join the device sends its safety ID and local block list to the room service; the service uses them only for the live admission decision and keeps them only with the live room socket state. Another participant receives only the safety ID needed for blocking and never receives the device's block list. Clearing app/site data or reinstalling resets the local safety ID and block list. The saved host-control token is removed from that device after a successful sign-out or account deletion so another account on the same device cannot inherit room administration. For each still-live room created by an account, the account service keeps a bounded internal room routing ID and its expiry only so account deletion can terminate that room. This record is not the participant link or host-control bearer, contains no peer identity or conversation content, and is removed no later than the room's 24-hour expiry or successful account deletion. A private abuse report retains its category, platform, time, and a non-reversible public room reference for up to 30 days. While the reported room can still be closed, the report also carries an internal non-invite room routing ID used only for moderator closure; that routing ID and its room-expiry value are removed when the room expires, no later than 24 hours after the room was created. The participant link and participant-safety IDs are never stored in the report. Infrastructure providers may retain limited security and operational logs under their own policies.

Abuse prevention. Cloudflare supplies the request source IP at the edge. Lingua Relay computes a SHA-256 digest of the Cloudflare-provided source IP before selecting a per-action rate-limit object. The quota record itself contains only a window start and request count, is not joined to the signed-in account, is not used for advertising or tracking, and is deleted automatically when that quota window ends, no longer than 24 hours after it starts. The raw source IP is not written into the application's quota record.

Accounts and sign-in

Signing in. Only the person who starts a call signs in. Anyone they invite joins the room by opening the link, with no account and no sign-in. We never create, receive, or store a password: sign-in is handled by the OAuth providers enabled for that release. The source supports Google and Apple plus optional Facebook, and the account screen offers only providers that are fully configured. What reaches us after sign-in is an account identifier, an email address, and a display name. The provider's own identifier is hashed on arrival and stored only as a derived account ID that cannot be turned back into the provider identifier.

What an account holds. The account stores the email address, display name, sign-in provider, usage totals, recent usage rows for call minutes, chat messages, and translated-voice phrases, and the bounded internal routing references for its still-live rooms described above. Each usage row carries an opaque reference to the room it came from — never the room link, a participant name, participant-safety ID, or message, caption, audio, or video content. When you sign out, the service may temporarily retain a one-way SHA-256 digest of that specific signed-out session token so a copied token cannot be replayed. The token itself is not stored in this revocation record. Version 1.0 does not sell credits or accept in-app payments.

How long. The account profile and aggregate usage totals last until you delete the account. Usage rows are kept for 90 days, and only the most recent 200 are retained. Account-owned room routing references last only while those rooms can still exist, never later than 24 hours after room creation. A signed-out session digest is kept only until that session token's original expiry, no longer than 30 days from sign-in, and is removed sooner if you delete the account.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, from the app or from this site: sign in, open the account controls, and choose Delete account. Before deletion reports success, Lingua Relay closes every still-live room owned by that account so its participant invitation can no longer be used. If the service cannot confirm closure of all owned rooms, deletion does not report success and the account remains available so the operation can be retried. After room closure succeeds, deletion removes the account profile, the display name and email we received, usage totals, every usage row, account-owned room routing references, any signed-out session revocation digests, and the saved host-control token from that device. Device-local participant safety blocks are separate from the account and remain until app/site data is cleared so deleting one host account does not silently remove the user's safety choices.

Your controls

You can mute, disable video or translated voice, leave a room, block the current participant on this device, submit a private category-only report, or close a room from the host dashboard. Reporting also adds the current participant to the device-local block list; in the installed app, a durably accepted report also closes the current reported room. Room links and host controls expire after 24 hours unless the room is closed sooner.

Children

Lingua Relay is not directed to children under 13.
