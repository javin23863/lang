Privacy

Effective August 14, 2026

Lingua Relay is operated by the javin23863 Lingua Relay project.

What the app processes

Lingua Relay processes microphone audio to create live captions and translations. Camera and natural call audio travel between participants through encrypted WebRTC connections and may use a relay when a direct connection is unavailable.

Where processing happens

Cloudflare provides the room, signalling, relay, and private abuse-report inbox. Modal runs speech recognition, translation, and optional translated-voice processing.

What we keep

The service has no advertising profile, transcript history, or conversation database. Audio, video, captions, and translated voice are not intentionally stored. A host-control token and language choices are kept on the user's device. A private abuse report retains its category, platform, time, a non-reversible public room reference, and an internal non-invite room routing ID used only to let a moderator close the active reported room. The participant link is not stored. Reports are deleted after 30 days. Infrastructure providers may retain limited security and operational logs under their own policies.

Accounts and sign-in

Signing in. Only the person who starts a call signs in. Anyone they invite joins the room by opening the link, with no account and no sign-in. We never create, receive, or store a password: sign-in is handled by Google, Apple, or Facebook, and what reaches us is an account identifier, an email address, and a display name. The provider's own identifier is hashed on arrival and stored only as a value that cannot be turned back into it.

What an account holds. An account record (email, display name, sign-in provider), a credit balance, and usage counts: call minutes, chat messages, and translated-voice phrases. Each usage row carries an opaque reference to the room it came from — never the room link, never the room's name, and never any message, caption, audio, or video. There is no transcript, no recording, and no history of what was said.

How long. The account record and balance last until you delete the account. Usage rows are kept for 90 days, and only the most recent 200 are retained.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, from the app or from this site: sign in, open the account menu, and choose Delete account. Deletion is immediate and removes the account record, the display name and email we received, the credit balance, the usage totals, and every usage row. Rooms you created keep running until their links expire; nothing they meter afterwards is stored, because there is no longer an account to store it against.

Your controls

You can mute, disable video or translated voice, leave a room, report and block a room, or close a room from the host dashboard. Room links and host controls expire after 24 hours.

Children

Lingua Relay is not directed to children under 13.

Questions

Use the support page. Do not include private conversation content in a support request.
