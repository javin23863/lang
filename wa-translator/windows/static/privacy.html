Privacy

Effective August 25, 2026

Lingua Relay is operated by the javin23863 Lingua Relay project.

What the app processes

Lingua Relay processes microphone audio to create live captions and translations. Camera and natural call audio travel between participants through encrypted WebRTC connections and may use a relay when a direct connection is unavailable.

Where processing happens

Cloudflare provides the room, signalling, relay, account service, and private abuse-report inbox. Modal runs speech recognition, translation, and optional translated-voice processing.

What we keep

The service has no advertising profile, transcript history, or conversation database. Audio, video, captions, chat text, and translated voice are not intentionally stored as conversation history. A host-control token and language choices are kept on the user's device. The saved host-control token is removed from that device after a successful sign-out or account deletion so another account on the same device cannot inherit room administration. The already-issued participant room remains independent and can continue until its normal expiry. A private abuse report retains its category, platform, time, and a non-reversible public room reference for up to 30 days. While the reported room can still be closed, the report also carries an internal non-invite room routing ID used only for moderator closure; that routing ID and its room-expiry value are removed when the room expires, no later than 24 hours after the room was created. The participant link is never stored in the report. Infrastructure providers may retain limited security and operational logs under their own policies.

Accounts and sign-in

Signing in. Only the person who starts a call signs in. Anyone they invite joins the room by opening the link, with no account and no sign-in. We never create, receive, or store a password: sign-in is handled by Google, Apple, or Facebook, and what reaches us is an account identifier, an email address, and a display name. The provider's own identifier is hashed on arrival and stored only as a derived account ID that cannot be turned back into the provider identifier.

What an account holds. The account stores the email address, display name, sign-in provider, usage totals, and recent usage rows for call minutes, chat messages, and translated-voice phrases. Each usage row carries an opaque reference to the room it came from — never the room link, a participant name, or message, caption, audio, or video content. When you sign out, the service may temporarily retain a one-way SHA-256 digest of that specific signed-out session token so a copied token cannot be replayed. The token itself is not stored in this revocation record. Version 1.0 does not sell credits or accept in-app payments.

How long. The account profile and aggregate usage totals last until you delete the account. Usage rows are kept for 90 days, and only the most recent 200 are retained. A signed-out session digest is kept only until that session token's original expiry, no longer than 30 days from sign-in, and is removed sooner if you delete the account.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, from the app or from this site: sign in, open the account controls, and choose Delete account. Deletion is immediate and removes the account profile, the display name and email we received, usage totals, every usage row, any signed-out session revocation digests, and the saved host-control token from that device. Rooms you created keep running until their links expire; nothing they meter afterwards is stored to the deleted account.

Your controls

You can mute, disable video or translated voice, leave a room, report and block a room, or close a room from the host dashboard. Room links and host controls expire after 24 hours.

Children

Lingua Relay is not directed to children under 13.

Questions

Use the support page. Do not include private conversation content in a support request.
