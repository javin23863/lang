Support

Call help

  • Check camera and microphone permission.
  • Reopen the invitation and choose the correct language.
  • Try Wi-Fi and cellular data separately.

Report abuse

During a call, open the call menu (⋯), choose a reason and tap Report & block this room. The retained report includes the category, platform, time, an opaque reference, and an internal routing ID used only for moderator closure. It does not include names, audio, video, captions, transcripts, chat text, or the participant link.

Product support

Open a public product-support request. Do not include an account email, room link, real name, phone number, transcript, recording, private-call screenshot, or other private account/conversation data.

Delete your account

Open Lingua Relay, sign in with the same provider you used before, open the account controls, and choose Delete account. It takes effect immediately and needs no message to us. If you cannot sign in, do not put your email address or other account identifiers in the public support tracker. A dedicated private product-support contact must be published here before store submission for access-loss account requests.

Immediate safety

Leave or close the room immediately. Lingua Relay is not an emergency service.
