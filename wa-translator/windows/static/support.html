Call help

  • Check camera and microphone permission.
  • Reopen the invitation and choose the correct language.
  • Try Wi-Fi and cellular data separately.

Report abuse

During a call, open the call menu (⋯), choose a reason and tap Report & block this room. The retained report includes the category, platform, time, an opaque reference, and an internal routing ID used only for moderator closure. It does not include names, audio, video, captions, transcripts, or the participant link.

Product support

Open a public product-support request. Do not include a room link, real name, phone number, transcript, recording, or private-call screenshot.

Immediate safety

Leave or close the room immediately. Lingua Relay is not an emergency service.
