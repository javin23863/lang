Delete your Lingua Relay account

Updated August 25, 2026

You do not need the mobile app to delete your account. Use Lingua Relay in this browser, sign in with the same provider you used to create the account, then choose Delete account in the account controls.

Open Lingua Relay account controls

What deletion removes

Deletion is immediate. It removes your account profile, display name and email address received from your sign-in provider, aggregate usage totals, and retained usage rows.

What is not an account record

A room you already created is independent of the account after creation. Its private link and host control expire no later than 24 hours after the room was created. If the room remains open after account deletion, later usage is not written back to the deleted account.

If you cannot sign in

Do not post your email address, account identifiers, room links, transcripts, recordings, or screenshots in the public issue tracker. A dedicated private product-support contact is required before store submission and will be published on the support page for access-loss deletion requests.

For full retention details, read the Privacy policy.
