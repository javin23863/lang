Delete your Lingua Relay account

Updated August 26, 2026

You do not need the mobile app to delete your account. Use Lingua Relay in this browser, sign in with the same provider you used to create the account, then choose Delete account in the account controls.

Open Lingua Relay account controls

What deletion removes

Before deletion reports success, Lingua Relay closes every still-live room owned by the account so those participant invitations stop working. If the service cannot confirm closure of all owned rooms, deletion does not report success and the account remains available so you can retry. After room closure succeeds, deletion removes your account profile, display name and email address received from your sign-in provider, aggregate usage totals, retained usage rows, and the short-lived internal room-routing references used for that shutdown.

What is not an account record

Device-local participant safety blocks are separate from the account and remain until app/site data is cleared. Room links and host controls normally expire no later than 24 hours after creation, but successful account deletion closes rooms owned by that account sooner.

If you cannot sign in

Do not post your email address, account identifiers, room links, transcripts, recordings, or screenshots in the public issue tracker. A dedicated private product-support contact is required before store submission and will be published on the support page for access-loss deletion requests.

For full retention details, read the Privacy policy.
