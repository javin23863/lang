Private communication

Lingua Relay provides private link-based calls and chats with automated captions, translation, and optional translated voice. You are responsible for sharing room links only with people you intend to invite. Version 1.0 has no public room directory, people search, discovery feed, or random matching.

Translation limitations

Machine transcription and translation can be delayed or wrong. Do not rely on the app for emergencies, legal advice, medical decisions, financial instructions, or any situation where an error could cause harm.

Acceptable use

Do not use Lingua Relay to harass, threaten, exploit, impersonate, distribute illegal material, or violate another person's privacy or rights. Leave, block, report, or close a room immediately if participation is unwanted.

Availability

The service may limit capacity, languages, voices, or call duration and may be unavailable during maintenance or provider outages.

Usage and payments

Version 1.0 does not sell credits or accept in-app payments. Recent usage may be shown as account activity and is not a bill or a stored conversation history.

Your account

An account is required to start a call; people you invite join without one. You may delete your account at any time from the account controls, in the app or on this site. Deletion removes the account record, usage totals, and usage rows. Device-local participant blocks are independent safety choices rather than account records and remain until app/site data is cleared.

Reporting and blocking

Use Block participant during a live room to store that participant's random pseudonymous safety ID in a bounded block list on this device and leave the room. On a later private-room encounter, Lingua Relay refuses admission when either participant presents a block relationship involving the same safety ID. The safety ID is not a guest account, public profile, credential, contact identifier, or searchable identity. It is installation-scoped: clearing app/site data or reinstalling resets the ID and local block list, and another installation has its own ID. Use the category-only report action when you also want to submit a private abuse report; reporting adds the current participant to the local block list, and in the installed app a durably accepted report also closes that reported private room so its invitation cannot continue or be re-entered. Never include room links, transcripts, or private media in a public support request.
