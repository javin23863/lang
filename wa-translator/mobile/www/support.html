Support

Before reporting a call problem

  • Confirm camera and microphone permission is enabled.
  • Leave the room, reopen the invitation link, and choose the correct language.
  • Try Wi-Fi and cellular data separately.

Request help or report abuse

Open a support request. Reports are public. Never include a room link, real name, phone number, transcript, recording, or screenshot of a private call.

Immediate safety

Leave the room or use the host dashboard to close it. Lingua Relay is not an emergency service.
