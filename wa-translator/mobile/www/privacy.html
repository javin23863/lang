Privacy

Effective August 14, 2026

What the app processes

Lingua Relay processes microphone audio to create live captions and translations. Camera and natural call audio travel between participants through encrypted WebRTC connections and may use a relay when a direct connection is unavailable.

Where processing happens

Cloudflare provides the room, signalling, and relay layer. Modal runs speech recognition, translation, and optional translated-voice processing. Room links and network metadata are processed to operate and protect each call.

What we keep

The service has no user accounts, advertising profile, transcript history, or conversation database. Audio, video, captions, and translated voice are not intentionally stored by Lingua Relay. A host-control token and language choices are kept on the user's device. Infrastructure providers may retain limited security and operational logs under their own policies.

Your controls

You can mute, disable video or translated voice, leave a room, or close a room from the host dashboard. Room links and host controls expire after 24 hours. The app can clear its saved room control when you close or replace a room.

Children

Lingua Relay is not directed to children under 13.

Questions

Use the support page. Do not include private conversation content in a support request.
